**New in fixed125:**

1. The intrinsic mod-four Fox–Wu identity
   \[
   \beta_1(\chi)=\chi\cup\chi=\chi\cup w_P,\qquad
   w_P=(\theta_P-1)/2\bmod2.
   \]
   On local Galois sectors, $w_P$ is the Kummer class of $-1$.
2. An exact formula for the entire mod-$2$ lifting filtration of **every** integral rank-one unit twist $\psi$, determined by the valuation and leading mod-$2$ residue of its single twisted Fox top-cell row. The canonical orientation is characterized by full liftability at every $2$-power level.
3. An integral $H^0/H^1/H^2$ Kummer–Tate computation for **every finite extension $K/\mathbf Q_2$**, retaining the root-of-unity torsion $q_K$.
4. The explicit $G_{\mathbf Q_2}(2)$ twisted differential and the exact cyclotomic-power formula $a(\theta^k)=2+v_2(k-1)$ for odd $k\ge3$.
5. A transfer-normalized **integral derived local Tate pairing** on the all-Sylow finite Fox carrier, without treating normalized corestriction as multiplicative.
6. An explicit note that the even-$q=2$ $f=\infty$ two-generator-image relation duplicates the one-generator-image boundary; the four families remain disjoint after normalization.

This is a single, self-contained TeX manuscript. The core finite-projective all-Sylow/Cayley–Hamilton existence and recursive-globalization proofs are retained. No uniform marked full-$G_K$ word presentation for arbitrary dyadic extensions is claimed.
